# Contributing

[docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) says how Pley is built and
tested, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) the rules every change keeps: the
checks to run, licensing, security, and how commits read.

## The license of a contribution

Pley is under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only, with
an additional permission for app stores ([LICENSE](LICENSE),
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)), and what you contribute is released under it
too. The Kodi add-on (`apps/kodi`) is the exception: contributions to it
come under its own license, the GPL version 2 or later.

Contributions are also made under the Pley
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). Contributors keep the copyright
in their work, which is released under Pley's license; the agreement also
grants Pley's maintainer the right to license contributions under other
terms, such as a commercial license, or with added permissions such as the
one for app stores. That right is the maintainer's alone.

Contributors from before the agreement have agreed to the same terms.

Opening a pull request accepts the agreement, and confirms that you wrote
what it contains, or have the right to contribute it under these terms.
