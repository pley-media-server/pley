# Contributing

[docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) says how Pley is built and
tested, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) the rules every change keeps: the
checks to run, licensing, security, and how commits read.

## The license of a contribution

Pley is under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only, with
an additional permission for app stores ([LICENSE](LICENSE),
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)), and what you contribute is released under it
too. The Kodi add-on (`apps/kodi`) is the exception: contributions to it
come under its own license, the GPL version 2 or later.

You also sign Pley's [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). You keep the
copyright in what you wrote, and everyone still gets it under Pley's
license; the agreement also lets Pley's maintainer, Houssam Haidar, offer
it under other terms, such as a commercial license, or add a permission
like the one for app stores. That right is the maintainer's alone.

The CLA bot asks you to sign on your first pull request: reply with the
sentence it gives, and it remembers you for the next ones. Signing covers
what you contributed before, too. Those who contributed before the
agreement existed have agreed to the same terms.

Opening a pull request confirms that you wrote what it contains, or have
the right to contribute it under these terms.
