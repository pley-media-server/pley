# Contributing

[docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) says how Pley is built and
tested, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) the rules every change keeps: the
checks to run, licensing, security, and how commits read.

## The licence of a contribution

Pley is under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only
([LICENSE](LICENSE), [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)), and what you
contribute comes under it too (`AGPL-3.0-only`). The Kodi add-on
(`apps/kodi`) is the exception: contributions to it come under its own
licence, the GPL version 2 or later.

Opening a pull request confirms that you wrote what it contains, or have
the right to contribute it under these licences.
