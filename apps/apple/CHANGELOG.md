# Changelog

Notable changes to Pley for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, newest first.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/)
and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Finds Pley servers on your network, keeps the ones you opened, and adds
  one by its address.
- Sign in with your browser on iPhone, iPad and Mac, or with a code and its
  QR code on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. Your sign-in stays in the Keychain
  on this device only.
- Choose who's watching from the household's profiles, with the profile's
  PIN when it has one.
- A tab bar on iPhone, a sidebar on iPad and Mac, and tabs across the top on
  Apple TV; your profile, servers and sign-out are under Me.
- Home shows your rows with their posters, moved through with the remote
  on Apple TV.
- Browse your libraries: sort, filter, jump by letter, and put libraries
  in your own order. Home has See all, and long press or right-click for a
  title's actions. Screens update when something changes on the server.
- Full title pages: seasons and episodes, cast and crew, extras and
  trailers, More Like This, media info, your rating, watched marks and My
  List; people's pages; artist and album pages.
- Collections and playlists: play or shuffle them, add titles to playlists,
  make, rename, reorder and delete playlists, and save a library's filters
  as a smart playlist.
- Search, titles first, then Live TV and people, with recent searches;
  Settings for quality, the next episode, subtitle style and grid size;
  History, Diagnostics and About. On Apple TV, choose who's watching at
  start (a profile with a PIN asks for it, even the one used last) or
  always start as one profile.
- On the Mac: File and View menus with shortcuts, Settings (⌘,), About
  Pley, hover and right-click. On Apple TV, a row puts the focus back on
  the tile you left it on.
- Sign out of a server, or forget it.
- Your token goes only to the server you signed in to: before it goes, the
  server proves it holds your sign-in's key, again after half an hour, on
  another network, or when the server didn't answer. When another device
  answers at its address, the token stays here and Pley shows what it saved,
  as it does for a server that's offline.
- Play movies and episodes: Play or Resume on an item's page, the system
  player on each device (its own window on the Mac, with keyboard
  shortcuts), and each file sent as this device plays it best, in HDR and
  Dolby Vision where the screen shows them.
- A Quality menu with Original, Automatic and each quality; Automatic
  lowers the quality when the video doesn't play smoothly and goes back up
  when it can.
- Resume where you left off, Skip Intro and Skip Credits, Up Next for the
  next episode, chapters, and an audio track choice the server remembers
  for the whole show.
- Subtitles, text and picture (PGS, VobSub, DVB) alike, drawn over the
  video in your caption style, with a sync control, and text subtitles'
  style in the player's menu too; picture subtitles never force a
  conversion, and a heavy one never holds up the screen or the remote.
  Find Subtitles… in the player searches online through your server and
  adds the subtitles you choose, which show at once.
- Playback speed from 0.5× to 2×, and a sleep timer for videos with Keep
  watching in its last 30 seconds.
- Picture in Picture on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac: from the player's
  button, and by itself when you leave the app on iPhone and iPad; its ✕
  ends the playback.
- Live TV on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV: the categories, the guide and
  the channel cards, favourites and recently watched, a programme's
  details, and the live player with channel up and down, a channel number
  typed or on Apple TV's number pad, AirPlay, and Watch here instead when
  every connection is in use. Search shows the channels it finds. Live TV
  stopped from another device, or its connection taken, stops at once and
  says why.
- Music: Play and Shuffle on albums, artists and music playlists, a track
  plays its album from there, and it plays on in the background and with
  the screen locked. A mini player and Now Playing (a window on the Mac,
  a tab on Apple TV) with Up next, Shuffle, a sleep timer and AirPlay; the
  lock screen, Control Center, headphones and the Mac's media keys play,
  pause and skip music and videos.
- Download movies, episodes and whole seasons on iPhone, iPad and Mac to
  watch without your server: as they are, or smaller (Settings →
  Downloads), in the background, on Wi-Fi unless you allow mobile data.
  They're in Me → Downloads, and play with their subtitles, Resume and Up
  Next while the server is away; what you watched reaches the server once
  it is back.
- When the server doesn't answer, the app opens on what it saved of your
  screens under "Pley is offline", with Downloads on iPhone, iPad and Mac,
  and reconnects by itself (Apple TV too).
- A video that loses the server plays what it has loaded and keeps trying
  to reach the server for 90 seconds, then plays on where it stood.
- Continue Watching on Apple TV's Top Shelf: with Pley in the top row,
  your titles in progress show with their pictures and progress, and one
  opens its page or plays.
- Pley's icon on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. On Apple TV it moves in
  layers as it's focused, and the Top Shelf shows Pley's icon and name
  until there's something to continue.
- Requests on iPhone, iPad and Mac: discover movies and shows in Discover
  or from Search, ask for them (picking a show's seasons), follow your
  requests, and hear when they're ready. Administrators approve and
  decline under Me, with a count on Me's tab.
- Requests on Apple TV, as on Android TV: a Discover tab (Your requests,
  or Requests for administrators, where you can't ask) with its count,
  posters to browse and ask for, Which seasons? and each request's actions
  in a panel down the side, Discover's titles in Search, and a pop-up when
  something you asked for is ready to watch.
- In Discover and Search's Discover titles, a movie or show a library has
  whole opens its library page, where it plays.
- A remote for your other devices on iPhone, iPad and Mac: a bar above
  the tabs, or a toolbar button on the Mac, shows what plays on them, and
  plays, pauses, seeks, skips, goes to the next or previous track (or
  channel) and stops it. Every Apple device, Apple TV included, obeys
  your remotes, the web's too, while Pley is open or music plays, and
  music paused here stays on them.
- Your remotes can change the audio and subtitles of a movie or an episode
  playing on an Apple device, and see which ones it plays.
- The remote lists all your other devices that are on, playing or ready
  to play, each with its kind: choose one for its controls, with Audio and
  Subtitles, and Next wherever there is one.
- Play on… sends a title, a show, an album, an artist, a track or a
  playlist (Watch on…, a Live TV channel) to another of your devices.
  Every Apple device, Apple TV included, plays what you send it as its own
  Play would, and says where it came from; on Apple TV the player's other
  notices show too.
- Your remotes can change the quality of a movie or an episode playing on
  an Apple device, as its Quality menu does, and see which one it plays.
- Play on… sends the audio and subtitles picked on a movie's or an
  episode's page, and the device plays it with them. A channel sent to a
  device already watching Live TV changes channel there, and what you send
  to an iPhone, iPad or Apple TV no longer waits behind a sheet open on it.
- The remote on iPhone, iPad and Mac: Audio, Subtitles and a new Quality
  are symbols beside Stop, which now asks first. Live TV shows how far
  along the programme is, its times and what's next, with a channel
  rocker in place of the seek bar and skips. A device playing nothing says
  "Send something here with Play on…"

### Changed

- Requests: a sign-in from before the app could ask says "Sign in again to ask for movies
  and shows" without a second line, and its button reads Sign in.
- A title's page on iPhone, iPad and Mac: Resume and From start share the
  row, with how far you got and the time left inside Resume; a line of
  facts no longer ends in a dot where it wraps, nor repeats the edition the
  Version picker shows; Version names the edition, with the picture, codec
  and size in its menu; and Audio and Subtitles show their choice on one
  line.
